How It Works

01
📊

Data Processing

We analyze 4,800+ movies from TMDB — extracting genres, cast, directors, keywords, and plot summaries.

02
🧠

TF-IDF Vectorization

Movie features are converted into numerical vectors using Term Frequency-Inverse Document Frequency.

03
📐

Cosine Similarity

We compute similarity scores between all movie pairs to find the closest matches to your selection.

04
⭐

Hybrid Ranking

Results are re-ranked using a popularity boost to surface high-quality, well-rated recommendations.
